Six disciplines, one contractor.

Each discipline below is backed by completed contracts on file. Many jobs touch more than one — a single hydropower access-road contract can call for rock excavation, gabion protection and plum concrete in the same agreement.

Road Construction & Periodic Maintenance

Sub-base and base course, bituminous penetration macadam, premix carpeting and asphalt concrete for national highways, Department of Roads divisions and municipal roads.

Hydropower Civil & Access Works

Access-road excavation in boulder-mix soil and hard rock, landslide clearance, and gabion and plum-concrete protection for run-of-river projects — now extending to project civil works under Super Machha Khola Lot III.

Industrial & Warehouse Construction

Earthworks, RCC foundations, masonry, roof structure and sheeting for factory warehouses — including three contracts inside Unilever Nepal's Hetauda premises.

Structural Steel Fabrication

MS beam, column, truss and chequered-plate fabrication and erection for factory mezzanines, warehouse roofs and corridor fencing.

Urban Drainage, Footpath & Corridor Works

RCC hume-pipe drainage, flagstone and interlock-block footpaths, and corridor upgrades for Kathmandu Metropolitan City and the Tokha and Lahan municipalities.

Fencing, Gabion & Retaining Works

River-corridor fencing, gabion boxes and stone-masonry retaining structures for slope stabilisation and site security.

Under our Class "A" license we also bid on building construction, water supply and transmission-line civil works.

Incorporated 2020. Built on roads, now building beyond them.

Sulav Infra Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated in Kathmandu in November 2020 and holds a Class "A" construction license from the Ministry of Physical Infrastructure and Transport — Nepal's highest contractor grade, required to bid on the country's largest public works.

We started in road construction and periodic maintenance for the Department of Roads and Kathmandu-valley municipalities. Within a year we'd taken on our first joint venture and our first private-sector client. That base has since extended into hydropower access infrastructure — excavation, gabion, and landslide clearance along powerhouse corridors — and into industrial construction, including structural steel and warehouse work for Unilever Nepal.

Many of our largest contracts run as joint ventures, where we've held anywhere from 25% to 60% share alongside partners like Uma Infra Builders, Durpana Nirman Sewa, and Dhami Construction — work that shows up on record with the Department of Roads and Office of the Company Registrar. Since 2020, we've moved from single-ward municipal contracts to national-highway maintenance and 20+ MW hydropower access roads, without ever leaving our original road-construction base behind.

Our stated vision is to support Nepal's infrastructural development and to lead within our field by valuing innovation and resourcefulness; our mission is to win work at competitive pricing while keeping site conditions safe and delivering quality within a reasonable time frame — the same standard we hold whether the employer is a rural municipality or a multinational factory.

  • Certified on completion, not just on paper

    Signed completion certificates from the employing agencies are on file for our certified contracts and available on request.

  • Equipment we own, crews we retain

    Excavators, rollers, and transport run in-house rather than fully subcontracted, which keeps schedules in our control.

  • Comfortable in joint ventures

    We've partnered on large public contracts where coordination and shared accountability were part of the brief, not an afterthought.

  • Government and corporate, side by side

    The same site discipline that satisfies a Department of Roads inspection has also passed Unilever's factory-premises standards.
